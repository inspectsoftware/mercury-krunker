**Pre-release.** An update to [v0.3.0](https://github.com/inspectsoftware/mercury-krunker/releases/tag/v0.3.0); its notes list everything Mercury Client does.

- **Mercury looks like part of Krunker:** its windows, panels, toasts and widgets now use Krunker's own font, window
  greys, buttons and switches instead of a separate dark theme.
  - The ranked rating sits beside the Ranked button as a plate with the button's own green border.
- **Classic menu, tidied:** the menu buttons are an even block of tiles, six to a row, clear of the chat and the play
  buttons.
- **Start as:** F8 → General → Start as: Maximized, Borderless fullscreen or Windowed.
- **Discord:** your profile shows "Playing Krunker.io" with the mode and map.
- **Fewer settings:**
  - The death screen fix is always on.
  - The five icon switches became the one Mercury icons switch.
  - The Discord application ID is built in.
  - The F11 fullscreen hotkey is gone; use Start as instead.
