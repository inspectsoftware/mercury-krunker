**Pre-release.** A small update to [v0.3.0](https://github.com/inspectsoftware/mercury-krunker/releases/tag/v0.3.0); its notes list everything Mercury Client does.

- **No more FPS drop on the death screen:** Krunker slides the death screen's bottom bar and the chat in over 1.5 s
  in a way that makes Chromium redraw them every frame. With uncapped FPS the game dropped to 52-82 FPS while they
  slid in (measured on our test PC; 457-485 FPS without the slide). Mercury now shows them at once.
  - On by default: F8 → Performance → Fix death screen FPS.
- Also in v0.3.2: the aim freeze fix (other players no longer freeze while you shoot with uncapped FPS).
