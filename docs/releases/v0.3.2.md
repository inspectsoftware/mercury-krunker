**Pre-release.** A small update to [v0.3.0](https://github.com/inspectsoftware/mercury-krunker/releases/tag/v0.3.0); its notes list everything Mercury Client does.

- **Aim freeze fix built in:** other players no longer freeze while you shoot with uncapped FPS.
  - The cause: Chromium puts frames ahead of the game's network messages while you move the mouse with the left
    button held. Mercury takes "left button held" out of the raw mouse moves Chromium reads. Your clicks and your
    movement reach the game exactly as before.
  - It's on by default: F8 → Engine → Aim freeze fix. It needs Raw mouse input (F8 → Input), which is on by default.
  - Krunker's own "Possible fix for jittery/freezing aiming" setting stays on too.
