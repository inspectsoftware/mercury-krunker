**Pre-release.** A small update to [v0.3.0](https://github.com/inspectsoftware/mercury-krunker/releases/tag/v0.3.0); its notes list everything Mercury Client does.

- **Discord Rich Presence works out of the box:** your profile shows "Playing Mercury Client" with the Mercury icon,
  plus your mode, map, class and time left.
  - Turn it off: F8 → Social → Discord Rich Presence.
  - Change it: put your own application ID there.
- **Release notes:** they now come from `docs/releases`, and releases start as pre-releases.
